Everything you need between two charges

Range Pilot sits next to your car's own estimate and gives you the number you can actually trust.

Learns your real consumption

Correct the battery % when your car shows a different number — the app learns and gets more accurate every drive.

Reacts to your speed

Motorway at 130 km/h costs much more than 90. Range and consumption update live as you drive.

Can I make it?

Enter a destination and see the battery % you'll arrive with — including hills along the route.

Weather aware

Below 10 °C an EV uses 20–30% more. Range Pilot tells you and keeps season-specific consumption for city, mixed and highway.

Tunnels & lost GPS

When the signal drops in a tunnel, the distance is recovered from your position when GPS returns — no lost kilometres.

Charging costs & stats

Log your charges and every trip shows what it really cost. Weekly and monthly statistics show where your energy goes.

How it works

Add your car

Name and battery size — that's all it needs.

Start a drive

Set your battery %, tap Start. Range, consumption and 10% = km update live.

Correct now and then

When your car's % differs, adjust it. Range Pilot learns and gets sharper every trip.

Drive with the number you can trust.

Free to use in your browser. Android and iPhone apps coming soon.
