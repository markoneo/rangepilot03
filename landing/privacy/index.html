Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Range Pilot app and website (rangepilot.net, app.rangepilot.net and the Range Pilot mobile apps) collect, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible

Artcomp Marko Keder s.p., Gozdna pot 10, 6240 Kozina-Hrpelje, Slovenia (VAT SI81926561) is the controller of your personal data. Contact: info@artcomp.net.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address and an encrypted password, used to sign you in.
  • Car settings — car name, battery capacity, preferred consumption, reserve and price settings.
  • Trip data — for each drive: start and end time, distance, duration, battery % at start and end, consumption, speed samples, minimum/maximum speed, outside temperature and elevation figures. Saved so you can see your history and statistics.
  • Location — while a drive is running, the app uses your device's GPS to measure distance and speed, including in the background when the screen is off (only if you allow it). Your route is not stored as a full GPS track. During a drive, the last known position is saved so a drive can continue after the app restarts; it is deleted when the drive ends. When GPS is lost (for example in a tunnel), the start and end points of the gap are used to calculate the road distance.
  • Destinations — if you use "Can I make it?", the address you search for and your current position are sent to Google Maps to find the place and calculate the route.
  • Charging log — charges you add (battery %, kWh, cost, place) are stored only on your device.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app: range, consumption learning, trip history and statistics (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Services we use

  • Supabase — account sign-in and database for your trips and settings.
  • Google Maps Platform — address search, routes and travel time for "Can I make it?".
  • Open-Meteo — outside temperature and terrain elevation for your position (no account data is sent).
  • OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM) — road distance after a GPS gap.
  • Netlify — hosting of the website and web app.

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us.

How long we keep it

Your account, settings and trips are kept until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete single trips in the app at any time. When you delete your account, all of this data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, and object to processing. Write to info@artcomp.net. You can also complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si).

Location permission

You can allow location "only while using the app" or "all the time". "All the time" lets the app keep counting kilometres when the screen is off or the app is in the background during a drive. Location is only used while a drive is running. You can change this permission any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Range Pilot is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
