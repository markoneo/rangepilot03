Range Pilot has no accounts

All your trips, settings and charges are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  • Android / iPhone app: uninstall the app, or open the phone's Settings → Apps → Range Pilot → Storage → Clear data.
  • Web app: clear the site data for app.rangepilot.net in your browser settings.
  • Single trips: in the app, Trips → the delete icon next to a trip.

Backup files you saved yourself are not touched — delete them wherever you stored them.

Accounts from early test versions

Early test versions used sign-in accounts. To have such an account and its saved trips deleted, e-mail info@artcomp.net from the e-mail address you signed in with, subject "Delete my Range Pilot account". We delete it within 30 days.
