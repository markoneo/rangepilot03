Range Pilot by Artcomp Marko Keder s.p.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@artcomp.net with the subject "Delete my Range Pilot account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Range Pilot, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner).
Email deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • Your car settings and preferences
  • All saved trips and statistics
  • Any drive in progress

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Anonymous technical records of GPS-gap distance calculations (no email, no account) may be kept to improve distance accuracy. Charges you logged are stored only on your phone — they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Delete only some data

You can delete individual trips any time in the app (Trips → the delete icon next to a trip). To delete other specific data without closing your account, email info@artcomp.net.
